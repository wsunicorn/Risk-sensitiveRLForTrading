Project Overview

This platform demonstrates the comparison between two reinforcement learning approaches for algorithmic trading: PPO (Risk-Neutral) and CVaR-PPO (Risk-Sensitive).

PPO (Risk-Neutral)

Baseline approach maximizing expected returns without explicit risk management

Actor-Critic architecture • 256 hidden units • 100 episodes
CVaR-PPO (Risk-Sensitive)

Risk-sensitive approach with CVaR constraint managing tail risk

Penalizes worst 15% returns • Adaptive lambda • Downside protection
Sortino-PPO

Risk-adjusted approach focusing on downside volatility

Sortino ratio optimization • Risk-adjusted returns
